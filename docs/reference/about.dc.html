About us

A small Nottingham team, not a national franchise.

A small family-run team working across NG1 to NG16 — the city centre, West Bridgford, Lenton, Beeston, Sherwood, Arnold, Bulwell, Wollaton and the districts in between. We have no plans to spread thinner than that.

Local, and staying that way

Every clean is carried out by a vetted, DBS-checked cleaner working to our checklist. Not a rota of strangers — you will always know who is coming to your home.

We quote a fixed price before we start, and that is the number you pay. If we arrive and the property is in worse condition than described, you hear what it will cost before we do the work — never afterwards on an invoice.

The small print, up front

Booking, cancellation and valuables

Three policies worth reading before you book, so nothing between us has to be argued about later.

Booking

Tell us the date you want and we will confirm it, or offer you the nearest slot we can genuinely keep. Then choose how you pay: in full by card before the clean, or half up front to secure the slot with the balance on the day. Either way the amount is the fixed price you already agreed.

Cancellation

Move or cancel up to 24 hours before, free of charge. Inside 24 hours, or if we cannot get in on the day, we charge half the visit.

Valuables and breakages

Please put cash, jewellery and anything irreplaceable away before we arrive. If we break something we tell you the same day and our £1m insurance covers it — we will not hide it and hope you miss it.
